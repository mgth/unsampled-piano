A piano is a machine, and you hear it working: the key bottoming on the keybed, a damper
landing on a ringing string, the pedal mechanism. Sample libraries record those noises
separately and mix them in; here they are **impulses into the instrument itself**, so they
ring through the same soundboard, reach the same microphones and fill the same room as the
notes — and they change with the instrument.

| Noise | Where it goes | When |
|---|---|---|
| Keybed knock | into the soundboard, at the key's bridge region | when the hammer strikes; grows with its speed |
| Damper landing | into the string modes, at the damper | when a damper falls on a ringing string; scaled by release velocity |
| Pedal knock | into the soundboard, two regions near the middle | when the sustain pedal moves; grows with its speed |

<Knocks />

All of them are **live**: set any to 0 to remove it. `damper_mass` and `damper_speed` together
make the momentum kick a landing damper gives the string; `damper_landing` and `knock_time`
are how long the pushes last, so how dull they sound.
